Session logins

Wise Old Man can infer that a session ended, and cannot see one begin at all - the hiscores do not move until logout. Dink reports both ends as they happen, so a session that crossed midnight is credited to both days rather than landing whole in the ten minutes we noticed it in.

Each player installs Dink from the RuneLite Plugin Hub, then pastes their own URL below into the setting called Custom Metadata Handler. That field is the only one they need - the Discord webhook box above it can stay empty. Ask them to untick Send Discord Profile and Send Clan Name while they are there; we throw both away, but there is no reason to send them.

A URL is a password. Send it to that player and nobody else, and issue a new one to retire a leaked one.

Optional. A player can also send what happens during a session. Put the same URL in Primary Webhook URLs as well - not instead, the metadata field is what carries the logins - and tick Collection Log, Level Up, Kill Count, Quests, Achievement Diary, Combat Achievements, Pets and Deaths. Collection slots and boss counts then land on the minute they happened rather than at the next reading; quests, diaries, tasks, pets and clog slots join the Milestones feed; deaths and pets bring a screenshot to the Gallery. Set Kill Count Interval to 1 for every kill; it ships at every fiftieth. The counts below are session events, then what arrived during play.
